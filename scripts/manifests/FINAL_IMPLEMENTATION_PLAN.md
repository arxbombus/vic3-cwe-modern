# CWE Era 11–20 Technology Extension — Final Implementation Plan

**Purpose:** implementation-ready specification for repairing the genuinely zero-effect Era 1–10 technologies and extending all 26 current CWE technology chains through Era 20 (~2300), while respecting the current building/PMG architecture.

## 0. Non-negotiable compatibility rules

1. **Never add, restore, or extend a deprecated / unused PMG.** Source definitions are not evidence of gameplay use; a PMG must be attached to a current building.
2. **Never repair a dangling CWE PM.** If a PM is defined but absent from its active PMG (e.g. `pm_steel_goods_manufacturing_3`) or referenced but undefined (e.g. old advanced artillery/tank/warplane PMs), ignore it completely.
3. **All new production methods use the `pm_modern_...` prefix.** The new PM is appended to the existing active PMG; no existing PM is overwritten.
4. **Era 1–10 repair is modifier-only.** No new PM, unit, ship, or mobilization unlock may be injected into an existing CWE technology.
5. **Era 11–20 is where all new unlockable content lives.**
6. **Employment never falls further after Era 10.** Lower-strata jobs may be replaced by engineers, but total employment is held constant. Middle/high/special strata are not promoted.
7. **Productivity compensation must follow the building’s real output domain.** For ordinary goods-producing buildings, a composition-changing PM may add `building_modifiers.unscaled.goods_output_mult = 0.1`, and an already-middle-strata employment PM may add `goods_output_mult = 0.2`. Never use this blindly on non-goods buildings: Universities scale innovation/qualifications; Government Administration scales bureaucracy/tax/public-service/security effects; Construction Sectors scale construction output.
8. **No accidental resurrection by prerequisite.** New PMs may only require active PMs/PMGs/laws/technologies. Never reference a deprecated PM as an unlock requirement.
9. **Source-first regeneration.** The generator must re-read the user's current CWE source every run. The manifest below expresses design intent; baseline stats/direct modifiers are read from source wherever this plan says “continue”, “clone highest active”, or “repeat current package.”

## 1. Scope and deliverables

- **18 Era 1–10 compatibility fixes** by `INJECT`.
- **260 new technologies**: 26 chains × 10 eras.
- **592 new production methods**, of which **409 are Goods PMs**.
- **30 new army/air combat units**: 10 infantry, 10 air, 10 artillery.
- **52 new ships**: 50 main naval combatants + 2 troop ships.
- **10 new mobilization options**.
- **75/75 active Goods PMGs accounted for**: 74 receive direct future PMs; the legacy communication PMG is an intentional sibling exception because the same Communications Network building receives ten future post-internet PMs in `pmg_internet_infrastructure`.
- Full English localization for every new technology, PM, unit, ship, and mobilization option.
- A reusable source-reading Python generator for Era 11–20 and static modifier-only patch files for Era 1–10.

Era 11 = 2100–2119, 12 = 2120–2139, 13 = 2140–2159, 14 = 2160–2179, 15 = 2180–2199, 16 = 2200–2219, 17 = 2220–2239, 18 = 2240–2259, 19 = 2260–2279, 20 = 2280–2299.

## 3. Global PM/stat construction rules

### GLOBAL-EMPLOYMENT

For every future PM: do not reduce total employment below the Era-10/reference PM. Convert only lower-strata clerks/farmers/shopkeepers/machinists/laborers to engineers. Officers, soldiers, slaves, capitalists, aristocrats, academics, bureaucrats and existing engineers are never converted. On ordinary goods-producing buildings, composition-changing PMs use `goods_output_mult=0.1` and already-middle-strata employment PMs may use `0.2`. On non-goods buildings, apply an equivalent productivity improvement to the actual output domain instead; do not add an inert goods multiplier.

### PARTIAL-GOODS

For a partial/single Goods PMG, the baseline is the highest production method that is both defined AND actually listed in the active PMG. Never use a dangling definition. With N future tiers, scale every positive goods_input_* and goods_output_* from baseline by evenly spaced factors ending at 2.0x: N=3 -> 1.333/1.667/2.0; N=4 -> 1.25/1.5/1.75/2.0; N=5 -> 1.2/1.4/1.6/1.8/2.0; N=10 -> 1.1..2.0. Preserve non-goods mechanics unless a chain rule overrides them.

### AG-GRAIN/FABRIC/FRUIT/RUBBER

Per future era over PM10: +1 industrial chemicals input, +1 oil, +0.5 industrial robots, +0.5 software, +100 output of the primary good.

### AG-COFFEE/TEA/TOBACCO

Per future era over PM10: +1 industrial chemicals, +1 oil, +0.5 industrial robots, +0.5 software, +50 primary output.

### AG-WINE/OPIUM

Per future era over PM10: +2 industrial chemicals, +1 oil, +0.5 industrial robots, +0.5 software, +50 primary output.

### AG-FISH

Per future era: +1 industrial chemicals, +1 industrial robots, +1 oil, +0.5 software, +0.5 steamers, +100 fish output.

### AG-MEAT

Per future era: +1 electricity, +10 grain, +1 industrial chemicals, +0.5 industrial robots, +0.5 software, +10 fabric, +100 meat output.

### MINE-COAL

Per future era: +2 electricity, +2 industrial robots, +0.5 software, +50 coal output, +1 pollution. Do NOT reintroduce oil input removed by the current progression.

### MINE-IRON

Per future era: +2 electricity, +2 industrial robots, +2 oil, +0.5 software, +100 iron output, +1 pollution.

### MINE-GOLD/SULFUR

Per future era: +2 electricity, +2 industrial robots, +2 oil, +0.5 software, +50 primary output, +1 pollution.

### MINE-LEAD

Per future era: +2 electricity, +2 industrial robots, +2 oil, +0.5 software, +50 lead output, +1 gold by-product, +1 pollution.

### MINE-OIL

Per future era: +0.5 industrial robots, +0.5 software, +25 oil output, +1 pollution.

### LOG-WOOD

Per future era: +1 industrial robots, +1 oil, +0.5 software, +200 wood output, +1 pollution.

### AUTO-SERVICE

From PM10: +0.5 communication services, +1 electricity, +1 software, +0.5 computers, +1 pollution per era. Generic/shopkeeper staffing converts 100/1000 jobs to engineers per era; professionals stay 1000 engineers.

### AUTO-MANUFACTURING

From PM10: +0.5 communication, +0.5 electricity, +0.5 software and +2 pollution per era; generic/unskilled also +0.5 industrial robots, machinery uses +0.5 engines. Keep 1000 jobs and convert 100 lower-strata workers to engineers per era.

### ORG-LAND

From PM10: +0.5 corporate services per era; +1 transportation for agriculture/logging/mining/oil, +0.5 for manufacturing. Keep baseline total workers and convert 10% of baseline lower-strata employment to engineers each era.

### ORG-SERVICES

No mandatory new goods inputs. Keep 1000 jobs; clerks/shopkeepers convert by 100 per era, professionals remain 1000 engineers.

### COMM-NET

From pm_very_fast_internet_services: +0.2 professional services input, +25 communication services output and +1 pollution per era. Preserve computer/electricity/software values unless source changes. Keep 500 jobs and convert 50 clerks to engineers per era.

### UNIVERSITY-TECHNIQUE

From pm_technique_10_building_university: +1 communication services, +1 electricity, +1 software and +1 innovation per era.

### CONSTRUCTION

Future construction-output tiers use 1.2/1.4/1.6/1.8/2.0x PM5 material inputs; country_construction_add=7/8/9/10/11 and state_construction_mult=.04/.05/.06/.07/.08. Future size tiers add +2 transportation, +2 communication, +1 industrial robots, +1 computers, +2 software per tier and convert the fixed 500 laborers to engineers in 100-worker steps.

### AIR-DESIGN

Four future tiers from pm_large_jet_aircraft: each tier +1 aeroplanes input, +10 propulsion-energy input, +50 transportation output, +5 merchant marine, +10 infrastructure and +1 trade capacity; professional services remains 2. Staffing remains 1000 total and transitions machinists→engineers according to unlock era.

### ENERGY

Four-tier generation PMs clone the highest live PM and scale positive goods inputs/outputs 1.25/1.5/1.75/2.0. Future plant labor is engineer-only; previously engineer-only plants get output_mult .2, converted plants .1.

### SEA

Follow the explicit two-cycle 11–20 cadence. Port PM1 = 500 laborers+500 engineers; Port PM2 =1000 engineers. New ship-design and steamer tiers reach 1.5x then 2.0x their live baseline.

### NUCLEAR-TYPE

Type3–6: industrial chemicals 30/40/50/60; 2000 engineers +200 soldiers; prestige-from-army-power-projection .03/.04/.05/.06; WMD law; preserve nuclear timed modifier.

### NUCLEAR-ROCKET

Rocket2–5: advanced weaponry 8/11/14/17; oil 30/35/40/45; officers100; prestige .03/.05/.07/.09; WMD law. Gate each rocket on all nuclear types available by that point.

## 9. Reusable Era 11–20 generator — required behavior

The next implementation agent must create a reusable Python generator; the generator is part of the implementation, not optional.

### Inputs
- Current CWE `common/technology`, `production_methods`, `production_method_groups`, `buildings`, `combat_unit_types`, `ship_types`, mobilization definitions and localization.
- A versioned **design manifest** generated from this plan (the CSV files in this package are suitable source manifests).
- A small configuration section for paths/output and the separately approved Era 11–20 technology-cost policy.

### Source-discovery phase
1. Parse all current buildings and determine the actual active PMGs.
2. Parse each active PMG in declared order.
3. Parse PM definitions and mark a baseline PM usable only when **both present in the active PMG and defined**.
4. Parse Era 1–10 technologies and their current direct modifiers/prerequisites.
5. Parse live combat units, ships, mobilization options, and modifier keys.
6. Fail loudly if a manifest target PMG is no longer active, if a baseline disappears, or if an expected modifier key changes. Do not silently fall back to a deprecated object.

### Generation phase
1. Define Era 11–20 technologies from the manifest.
2. For “repeat current package” chains, copy the current per-tech modifier package from source rather than hard-coding a stale copy, except for explicit replacement packages (Population, Urbanization, Military Theory, Nuclear, Space, Sea direct tiers, Construction's future +0.50 rule).
3. Generate future PMs by the exact stat rules in `02_production_methods.csv` / this document.
4. Append PMs to existing active PMGs with `INJECT`; never replace the PMG.
5. Generate units/ships/mobilization options from the manifest and current source scaffolding.
6. Generate complete localization from the manifest.
7. Keep Era 1–10 compatibility fixes in a separate static `INJECT` file so regeneration never rewrites CWE originals.

### Prerequisites
- `tech_<chain>_11` requires the live `tech_<chain>_10`.
- Every later future tech requires the previous future tech in the same chain.
- If the live Era-10 tech contains additional non-self prerequisites, preserve them when extending unless they point to deprecated content.
- Never manufacture cross-chain prerequisites merely for flavor.

### Determinism
Running the generator twice against identical source must produce byte-identical generated content (apart from an optional timestamped report). IDs and unlock eras are manifest-controlled, never inferred from localization.
