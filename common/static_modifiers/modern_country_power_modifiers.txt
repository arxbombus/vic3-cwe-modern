### Final throughput ceilings are enforced across country + prosperous company + best relevant state trait.
### Bracket A: +150/+250/+350/+550 generic/supporting/secondary/primary.
### Bracket B: +75/+125/+185/+250; C: +60/+100/+140/+200; D: +45/+80/+100/+150.
### Bracket E: +35/+55/+75/+100; F: +25/+40/+55/+85; G: +18/+30/+40/+50.
### Enabling mechanics scale more gently; Aurelia is ~1.5x B outside direct productive specialization.
